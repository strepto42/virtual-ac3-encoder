Dolby Control Center - Quick Start
==================================

What it does: captures your PC's stereo or 5.1 surround audio, encodes it in real time to
DTS 5.1 (1536 / 768 kbps), Dolby Digital AC3 5.1 (640 / 448 kbps), or PCM Stereo 2.0, and
streams it over your optical (Toslink / S-PDIF) output to your AV receiver or soundbar --
software "Dolby Digital Live" & "DTS Connect".

Requirements
------------
1. A virtual audio device to capture audio. Install VB-CABLE (free):
       https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
   After installing, open Windows Sound settings:
     - Set "CABLE Input (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)" as your DEFAULT playback device.
     - (Optional) Configure Speakers > 5.1 Surround, or leave as Stereo and use Dynamic
       Surround 5.1 / Front Stage 3.1 upmixing in the Control Center.

2. An optical (S-PDIF / Toslink) output connected to your receiver's optical input.

How it runs
-----------
Dolby Control Center auto-starts minimized to the system tray at logon and supervises
the real-time audio engine (engine.exe). Double-click the tray icon to open the Control
Center and adjust:
  - Encoder Mode: DTS 5.1, Dolby Digital 5.1 (AC3), or PCM Stereo 2.0
  - Bitrate & Latency Preset (Extreme ~3ms to Relaxed ~35ms)
  - Stereo Upmix: Dynamic Surround 5.1, Front Stage 3.1 (Center + Sub), or Off
  - Individual 6-channel speaker trims (-60 dB to 0 dB) with live VU meters

Handy CLI commands (open a terminal in this folder)
---------------------------------------------------
       engine.exe --list      list audio input/output devices
       engine.exe --probe     show which outputs accept AC3/DTS passthrough
       engine.exe --version
